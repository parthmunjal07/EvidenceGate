## Presentation decisions

- Lists should favor time, record type, endpoints or domain, protocol, observed fact, direction, and route count. Type-specific details belong in the existing modal.
- Preserve source facts separately from canonical fields, visibility/quality, route decisions, and Results. Unknown and unavailable states remain explicit.
- Use the event's stated time and retain its source position; do not replace event time with ingestion or playback time.
- Keep full field dumps, identifiers, and provenance references collapsed. Do not surface raw payloads, local paths, model locators, plugin internals, or inferred roles in the primary view.
- Related-result navigation must show shared source context only; chronology or a shared source does not establish causality or a common actor.
