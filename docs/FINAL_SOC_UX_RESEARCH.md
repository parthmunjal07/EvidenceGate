# SOC / NDR UX research

Accessed 2026-09-26. Official product documentation only; these patterns inform interface structure and do not imply that EvidenceGate uses the same detection science.

| Product | Observed UX pattern | Why it works | EvidenceGate adaptation | Do not copy | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Microsoft Defender XDR / Sentinel | A queue opens into a contextual incident page with separate alert, activity, asset, investigation, and evidence views. Alerts are chronological and show why they are linked. | Analysts can move from triage to details without losing the selected incident context; chronology and linkage rationale are explicit. | Keep mechanism Result, family evidence episode, and factual Investigation distinct; show shared-source rationale and chronology in the selected investigation workspace. | Defender's correlated-incident and attack-story claims, entity graph, severity, and response actions. EvidenceGate's shared source observations do not establish an incident or attack story. | [Investigate incidents in the Microsoft Defender portal](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/defender-xdr/investigate-incidents) |
| Elastic Security | The alert flyout foregrounds highlighted fields, explanation, and investigation entry points; empty and implementation fields can be hidden, and raw JSON is a separate view. Preview flyouts preserve the table underneath. | Relevant facts are easier to scan while deeper technical detail remains available on demand and the analyst retains their place. | Put mechanism-specific observed facts and interpretation first; collapse technical metadata and source lineage; keep contextual navigation back to the originating family or investigation. | Elastic's risk scores, threat-intelligence enrichment, process graphs, and product-specific field model. | [View detection alert details](https://www.elastic.co/docs/solutions/security/detect-and-alert/view-detection-alert-details); [Elastic UI font settings](https://eui.elastic.co/docs/getting-started/theming/tokens/typography/font-settings/) |
| Splunk Enterprise Security Mission Control | One analyst queue presents findings, finding groups, and investigations; groups expand to their contributing findings, and selecting an item opens a detail side panel. A timeline supports triage. | The queue is the working entry point, while grouped items reduce repetition and retain access to their constituent records. | Make family evidence episodes the review queue rows; visually group repeated finding titles while preserving every immutable source Result and link. | Splunk urgency, assignment, disposition, incident workflow, and grouping semantics. EvidenceGate has no analyst status lifecycle or finding fusion. | [Mission Control overview](https://help.splunk.com/en/splunk-enterprise-security-8/user-guide/8.1/mission-control/overview-of-mission-control-in-splunk-enterprise-security); [Manage analyst workflows](https://help.splunk.com/en/splunk-enterprise-security-8/administer/8.0/mission-control/manage-analyst-workflows-using-the-analyst-queue-in-splunk-enterprise-security) |
| ExtraHop Reveal(x) | Detection cards foreground participants and detection-specific details. Investigation summaries use a chronological list with participant context; selecting a detection opens its details and navigation back to the investigation. | Network context and event time stay visible, and the analyst can move between the selected item and its broader workspace. | Show only observed, threat-specific network context; build a factual chronology from source Results and shared observations; keep a compact relationship summary instead of a graph. | Offender/victim roles, recommended investigations, attack maps, inferred campaigns, or risk scoring unless independently present in EvidenceGate evidence. | [Detections](https://docs.extrahop.com/current/detections-overview/); [Investigations](https://docs.extrahop.com/current/investigations-overview/); [Investigate security detections (PDF)](https://docs.extrahop.com/current/detections-investigate-security/detections-investigate-security.pdf) |

## Design decisions traced to product patterns

| Design decision | Source pattern | EvidenceGate adaptation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Relevant fields first | Elastic highlighted fields and hidden empty fields | Mechanism-specific context and facts lead each Result view. |
| Queue group to contextual detail | Splunk analyst queue and expandable finding groups | Family evidence is the queue-level episode; repeated child findings are visually grouped, with original Results retained. |
| Chronology with selected-item navigation | Microsoft incident activity and ExtraHop investigation timeline | Investigation workspace orders contributing family Results by observed time and links back to exact Results. |
| Separate evidence levels | Microsoft's distinct alerts, investigations, and evidence views | A Result is one mechanism output; Family Evidence is a read-only episode; an Investigation is factual joint-review context. |
